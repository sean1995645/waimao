# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary visitors are European and North American heating-product distributors, installers, project buyers, and smart-home brands evaluating thermostats and heating controls for resale, projects, or private-label programs.

## Product Purpose

HeatNexis presents and supplies heating-control products, helping buyers match thermostats and control hardware to electric floor heating, water floor heating, gas boiler, and connected-home applications, then request a project or OEM/ODM quotation.

## Positioning

HeatNexis connects consumer-friendly smart control and app experiences with a broad, project-ready heating-control catalog and OEM/ODM support.

## Operating Context

Buyers evaluate products by heating application, control protocol, voltage, display, integration requirements, target market, documentation, and customization scope.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Product catalog includes smart, programmable, mechanical, wireless, actuator, sensor, gateway, receiver, and control-center categories.
- Smart products may support Wi-Fi or Zigbee control and mobile app experiences where specified.
- The website is multilingual and must preserve current locale behavior.
- Commercial calls to action lead to the existing product catalog and contact workflow.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the HeatNexis name and existing logo assets.
- Homepage direction: a European/North American smart-home brand presentation with generous whitespace, real home imagery, prominent thermostat product views, app-control imagery, and application-led explanation.
- Required homepage narrative: Hero, Product Categories, Heating Solutions, Smart APP Control, Featured Products, OEM/ODM, Certifications, Why Choose Us, Global Partners, and Contact / Get a Quote.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing product catalog and specifications in `src/data/products.ts`.
- Existing product renders in `public/products/`.
- Existing home, product, about, and contact photography in `public/`.
- No verified customer logos, testimonials, certification numbers, market-share claims, or performance benchmarks are available; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Explain the system through real heating applications, not abstract technology claims.
- Make product selection and inquiry paths obvious to international buyers.
- Separate verified specifications from configurable OEM/ODM possibilities.
- Pair residential warmth with professional manufacturing clarity.
